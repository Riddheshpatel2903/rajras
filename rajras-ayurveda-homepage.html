What is
Swarnaprashan?

Swarnaprashan is a traditional Ayurvedic practice described in classical Ayurvedic literature. It is offered as a ritual for children, rooted in centuries-old texts on early-life care.

At Rajras, this practice is approached with care — bringing together traditional knowledge and thoughtfully selected ingredients for families who value tradition alongside modern life.

Thoughtfully Selected Ingredients

Suvarna Bhasma

A traditionally processed gold preparation referenced in classical Ayurvedic formulations for Swarnaprashan.

Ashwagandha

A well-known root in Ayurvedic tradition, long associated with strength and everyday vitality.

Brahmi

A herb referenced across classical Ayurvedic texts in connection with alertness and a calm mind.

Shankhpushpi

A traditional herb mentioned in classical Ayurvedic formulations intended for children.

Giloy

A climbing herb with a long-standing place in Ayurvedic preparations across generations.

Vacha

A traditional root used within classical Ayurvedic recipes prepared for infants and young children.
